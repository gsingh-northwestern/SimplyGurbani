About

Simply Gurbani brings the sacred scriptures of Sikhism to your fingertips with a beautiful, minimalist design. Whether you're performing your daily Nitnem, studying Gurbani, or seeking spiritual guidance, Simply Gurbani provides everything you need in one elegant app.

Features

  • Daily Hukamnama from Sri Harmandir Sahib
  • Complete Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji (1430 Angs)
  • Popular Banis including Japji Sahib, Rehras Sahib, and more
  • Gurmukhi text with English transliteration and translation
  • Bookmark your favorite verses
  • Reading position tracking
  • Search Gurbani by keywords
  • Offline caching for reading without internet
  • Beautiful, minimalist design

Privacy

Simply Gurbani respects your privacy. No personal data is collected, no analytics or tracking, and no advertisements. All your bookmarks and reading progress are stored locally on your device.
