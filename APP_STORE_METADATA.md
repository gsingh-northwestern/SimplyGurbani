## Description (4000 characters max)

```
Simply Gurbani brings the sacred scriptures of Sikhism to your fingertips with a beautiful, minimalist design.

Whether you're performing your daily Nitnem, studying Gurbani, or seeking spiritual guidance, Simply Gurbani provides everything you need in one elegant app.

DAILY HUKAMNAMA
Start each day with the divine command from Sri Harmandir Sahib (Golden Temple). The daily Hukamnama is updated automatically, bringing you fresh spiritual wisdom every morning.

COMPLETE SCRIPTURES
Access the complete Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji with all 1430 Angs (pages). Browse by page number, Raag, or Writer to find exactly what you're looking for.

POPULAR BANIS
Quick access to essential Sikh prayers:
- Japji Sahib
- Rehras Sahib
- Kirtan Sohila
- Sukhmani Sahib
- Anand Sahib
- Chaupai Sahib
And many more banis organized by category (Nitnem, Ceremonial, Major Works).

BEAUTIFUL READING EXPERIENCE
- Authentic Gurmukhi text with proper Unicode rendering
- English transliteration for pronunciation guidance
- English translations for understanding
- Adjustable font sizes for comfortable reading
- Larivaar mode for traditional continuous text display
- Clean, distraction-free interface

SMART FEATURES
- Bookmark your favorite verses and organize them in folders
- Continue reading from exactly where you left off
- Search Gurbani by keywords in English
- Jump directly to any Ang number
- Offline access with intelligent caching

DESIGNED WITH CARE
Simply Gurbani was designed with deep respect for the sacred texts. The warm, elegant color palette creates a peaceful reading environment while maintaining excellent readability.

No advertisements. No accounts required. No tracking. Just pure Gurbani.

Perfect for:
- Daily Nitnem practice
- Learning Gurmukhi pronunciation
- Studying Gurbani meanings
- Quick reference during Sangat
- Personal spiritual journey

Download Simply Gurbani and carry the wisdom of the Gurus with you wherever you go.

Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh!
```
